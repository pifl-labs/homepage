Find the same kana
Original PiFl Labs paper play, 17 October 2026.

Try together. No app or sign-up needed.
1. Point to the large shape on the left.
2. Find the same shape on the right. Point or circle it.
3. Swap turns, or stop whenever you like.

LOOK     FIND THE SAME SHAPE
あ       い    あ    う
い       う    あ    い
う       う    い    あ

Cover the other rows and try just one. You do not need to read Japanese.
This is a visual shape game, not a sound, handwriting or stroke-order guide.
Printed glyphs can look different from handwriting. This is not a test.
For an adult helper: the matching choices are middle, right, left.
