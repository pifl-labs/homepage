Yesternight Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Applies to: the Yesternight app (iPhone and Android)
Operator: PiFl Labs
Contact: privacy@pifl-labs.com

Yesternight is a dream journal you keep by voice. This policy explains where the app keeps your dream records and what it never does with them. Settings › Privacy in the app is a summary of this policy; this page has the details. Below, "this phone" means the phone you use the app on.

1. Personal information we collect: none

We, the operator (PiFl Labs), don't collect personal information. There's no sign-up or login. The app doesn't ask for your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts or advertising identifier, and we have no server that receives information from the app.

The app has no feature that connects to the internet. The Android app doesn't have the internet permission (INTERNET), and neither version includes analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The app doesn't send your dream recordings, transcripts, edited text, titles or feelings off the device. Device backups you turn on and exports you make yourself are covered in sections 5 and 6.

2. Where your dreams are kept

Your dream entries, recordings, and transcripts are stored on this phone. Transcription also happens on this phone, and the app never sends your dreams anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reports.

The app keeps the following in its own storage on this phone:

  • Dream records: recordings and their length, transcripts (including the transcription language) and edited text, titles, the date of each dream and when each entry was created and last edited (with the phone's time zone at that moment), feelings, vividness, lucid dream, nightmare and recurring dream marks, symbols you attached or removed, days marked "Can't remember", and when an entry was moved to the trash
  • Settings: appearance (dark or light), transcription language, whether to keep recordings after saving, morning reminder (on or off, time, days), app lock options (on or off, staying unlocked after a short break, hiding the screen), "Show a line from a past dream on Today" (on or off), whether you've finished the first-run guide, and a few notes the app keeps for itself (the date and app version of the latest backup copy, a note that this device can't transcribe, and the IDs of scheduled morning reminders)
  • Backup copies: two copies of your records that the app makes on this phone once a day (on days you open it) and after an app update, so it can restore your records if the file gets damaged. If the records file won't open, the app keeps that file instead of deleting it and restores from a copy.

The operating system opens this storage to this app only, so your dream records and recording files don't appear in your phone's file manager or in other apps. Each recording is kept as one file per dream in a recordings folder there.

3. Transcription

  • Recordings are turned into text only by speech recognition on this phone. On iPhone, the app uses on-device speech recognition (iOS 26 or later; on earlier versions, only for languages the device can recognize on-device). On Android, it uses on-device speech recognition (Android 13 or later). Nothing is handed to server-based recognition. On devices or languages without on-device transcription, the app keeps the recording without transcribing it, and you can still write the dream down.
  • The first time you transcribe a language, an on-device transcription model may be needed. The app asks first, and if you tap "Download model", your phone's operating system downloads the model. The operating system uses the internet for that download; your recordings and text are not sent.
  • When iPhone asks for speech recognition permission, the system alert may include Apple's standard wording that speech data may be sent to Apple. This app requests on-device recognition only.

4. Permissions and why they're used

Each permission is requested the first time you use the feature that needs it. If you don't allow it, other features such as writing a dream down still work, and you can change permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

iPhone

  • Microphone: to record your dreams. Requested the first time you record. Description in the permission alert: "The microphone is used to record the dreams you tell. The app doesn't upload your recordings."
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on this device. Requested after your first recording. Description in the permission alert: "Recorded dreams are transcribed on this device. Recordings and text are never sent to a server."
  • Face ID: to unlock the app lock. Requested when you turn the app lock on. Description in the permission alert: "Face ID is used to unlock the app lock. The app never receives your face data."
  • Notifications: to show morning reminders. Requested only when you turn morning reminders on.
  • Background audio (no permission alert): keeps a recording going if you lock the screen or go to the Home Screen while recording. It isn't used when you're not recording.

Android

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): to record your dreams. Requested the first time you record.
  • Microphone foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): keeps a recording going if the screen turns off or you switch to another app. It runs only while you're recording.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 or later): to show morning reminders and the recording notification ("Recording your dream"). Requested when you turn morning reminders on. Recording keeps going even if you don't allow it.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): sets your morning reminders again after the phone restarts (and after an app update).
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, or USE_FINGERPRINT on Android 8): to unlock the app lock with your fingerprint or face. This permission doesn't show a request.

The app doesn't request the internet (INTERNET), location, contacts, photos, camera or exact alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) permissions.

5. Device backups you turn on

iPhone

If you've turned on iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your iPhone includes your dream records and recordings in that backup. It's your own account's backup, not something the app sends. iCloud Backup is protected by Apple's standard protection (encrypted in transit and in storage), and with Advanced Data Protection turned on, it's encrypted so only you can open it.

Android

If you've turned on Google backup, your dream entries and settings are included only when the backup is encrypted with your screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password), so Google can't open them either. On Android 8, dream entries aren't included in Google backup; only the app's settings (no dream text) are, even without encryption. Recordings aren't included in Google backup. On Android 12 or later, moving directly to a new phone (by cable or phone-to-phone transfer) brings your recordings too; otherwise, use Export to move them.

Device backups are moved to your account or computer by the operating system, so we can't see them. The backup copies the app makes (section 2) are included in iPhone backups, but not in Android backups or phone-to-phone transfers (the new phone makes new ones). The original kept when a record file couldn't be opened (and the copies from that moment) is never deleted by the app: it moves with a phone-to-phone transfer on Android 12 or later, and the dreams in it stay on this phone until you delete the app.

6. Exporting is up to you

When you tap Export, the app makes a file with your dream entries and recordings and puts it where you choose (the Files app, a drive, a message, and so on). Dreams in the trash are included too. The file isn't password-protected and follows the protection of wherever you keep it, so keep it somewhere you trust.

  • So that sharing can finish, the file the app makes stays in the app's temporary folder on this phone for a while. It's deleted when you open the app an hour or more after it was made, or the next time you export. The temporary folder isn't included in device backups.
  • The export file contains your dream text and recordings along with marks such as dates, time zones and feelings.
  • Import: the app reads only the one export file you pick in the system file picker. It doesn't request access to all your files, and importing never deletes dreams or recordings on this phone.
  • Tapping "Copy text and close" in the editor copies that dream's text to your phone's clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.

7. Deleted dreams

  • Dreams you delete stay in the Trash for 30 days and are then deleted for good, along with their recordings. Before then, you can restore them or delete them for good right away.
  • Dreams you delete for good from the trash are removed from your records. They may stay for a while in the daily copy the app keeps on this phone and in device backups already made, and they stay in files you've already exported until you delete those files.
  • You can also delete just a recording and keep the text ("Delete recording" in the editor, or "Delete the recording after saving the text" in Settings).
  • Deleting the app deletes all dream records, recordings, copies and settings on this phone. Device backups already made and files you've exported have to be deleted separately.

8. App lock

iPhone

If you turn on the app lock in Settings, you unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode whenever you view your dreams, such as in the journal or a dream's details. Recording and saving work without unlocking. While the lock is on, the app screen is also hidden in the app switcher. Your iPhone checks your face or fingerprint; the app never receives face or fingerprint data.

Android

If you turn on the app lock in Settings, you unlock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) whenever you view your dreams, such as in the journal or a dream's details. Recording and saving work without unlocking. Turn on "Hide in recent apps" to hide them on the recent apps screen too. Your phone does the recognition; the app never receives fingerprint or face data.

9. Morning reminders, widgets and shortcuts

  • Morning reminders are notifications this phone shows at the time you choose. They aren't sent from a server, and they never contain your dream text. If you don't open the app for 4 weeks, morning reminders stop. Open the app again and they'll start back up.
  • The widgets, Control Center control and shortcut (iPhone), and the home screen widget, Quick Settings tile and app icon shortcut (Android) only open the app and start recording. They never show your dream text or pass it anywhere.
  • If you say a shortcut phrase to Siri on iPhone, Siri (Apple) processes what you say, and the app receives only a request to start recording.

10. Finding symbols

Symbols are found in your dream text on this phone, using the symbol dictionary built into the app. Your dream text isn't sent to a server or another company.

11. Sharing, processing and transfers abroad

Because we don't collect personal information, we don't share or sell it, entrust its processing to anyone else, or transfer it abroad. Device backups you turn on (section 5) and exported files you save somewhere you choose (section 6) follow the policies of those services.

12. Ads, analytics and tracking

There are no ads, and no usage analytics or tracking tools. The app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

13. Security

  • Dream records are kept in the app's own storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. We don't run a server, so nothing piles up or can leak on our side.
  • Dreams you delete for good are overwritten inside the records file so they don't remain in its free space.
  • The app doesn't write your dream text to logs.
  • The top of the Today screen may show one sentence picked from a dream you recorded in the past week (dreams marked as nightmares or with the "Scared" or "Anxious" feeling are skipped). Turn off "Show a line from a past dream on Today" in Settings to hide it. If the app lock is on, it also stays hidden while the app is locked.
  • If other people can use this phone, we recommend turning on the phone's screen lock and the app lock (section 8).

14. Children's privacy

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

15. Your rights

Everything the app stores is on this phone and in device backups you've turned on, so you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app at any time. We don't hold any information about you, so there's nothing for us to show you or delete. If you have questions, contact us at the address in section 17.

16. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the changes and a new effective date on this page. If we ever add a feature that sends information off the device, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent in the app before that feature is released.

17. Contact

Send questions about privacy to this email address: privacy@pifl-labs.com
